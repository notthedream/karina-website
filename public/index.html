GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick
answers.

Is Karina free?

Yes. Karina is free to download and has no subscription. A signed public installer is being prepared.

What does Karina play?

Karina plays local audio and video, using native macOS playback first and expanded-format support when needed. The exact formats and smoothness depend on the file and your Mac.

Does HDR or 8K60 work on every Mac?

No player can promise that. HDR and high-resolution playback depend on the codec, bitrate, hardware decoder, memory bandwidth, thermals and display path.

How does AI subtitle translation work?

Speech recognition creates time-coded captions on device. If you add an OpenAI API key and opt in, Karina can translate the subtitle text and nearby dialogue context into Thai or English. Audio and video are not sent.
