Local playback and data

Karina Media Player for macOS plays media files locally on your Mac. It does not collect, track, or sell personal data and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. Recent files and playlists are stored only on your device.

Network features

Optional SMB and DLNA browsing and sharing connect only to servers you choose on your own network.

AI subtitle translation

Optional AI subtitle translation is off by default. When you turn it on, subtitle text and nearby dialogue context are sent to the OpenAI API using your configured setup. Media files never leave your Mac.
