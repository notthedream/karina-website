Support

What formats does Karina support?

Karina plays audio and video, including MKV. Support depends on the file and your Mac.

Does Karina support subtitles?

Yes. Karina supports embedded subtitle tracks and SRT, VTT, ASS, and SSA subtitle files.

Which Macs are supported?

Karina requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later and supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

How do I report a bug?

Email notthedream@mail.grokbot.com with what happened, the steps to reproduce it, your macOS version, and your Mac model.
